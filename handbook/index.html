Start with the setup you have

Note your tank dimensions, light model and whether you add CO₂. If you do not know your lighting level, take a photo of the light or its label to the shop. A visual guess is not a reliable plant recommendation.

Keep in mind

Match the plant to your setup, then learn how it needs to be planted.

Check each plant’s needs

Look at its light and CO₂ requirements, mature size and planting method. Tropica’s Easy category is a useful starting point for less demanding choices. Still check the individual species against your tank.

  • Leave room for the plant’s mature height and spread.
  • Check whether it roots in the substrate, attaches to a surface or floats.
  • Consider the needs of any fish already in the aquarium.

Plan where it will grow

Anubias and Java fern have a rhizome: the thick stem from which roots and leaves grow. Keep this stem above the substrate. For rooted or stem plants, follow the instructions for that plant.

Bring a useful shortlist

Save the plants you like and bring your setup details. Ask the shop to help you compare suitable options before you order.

Questions to take to the shop

  • Does this plant suit my light and CO₂ setup?
  • How large will it grow?
  • How should I plant it?
